2. Homeowner (P-E "Karen") — DTA §2.1, §6.5(a–d), §6.9

  • HO-01 — Request a pro: pick a trade, describe the need, photos, address, draft and duplicate detection
  • HO-02 — Search for a provider or post a project
  • HO-03 — Compare, confirm and track contracted work
  • HO-04 — Manage approval, payment and communication with the pro
  • HO-05 — Verify contact and authority over the property; consent by purpose and channel; recipient cap shown
  • HO-06 — Book, reschedule or cancel a visit window; no-show recovery
  • HO-07 — "On the way" and ETA notices
  • HO-08 — Messages with the pro through WorkGig (contact relay, unified inbox)
  • HO-09 — Review estimate options; ask or propose changes; versioned revisions
  • HO-10 — Accept and sign the exact estimate version; optional deposit
  • HO-11 — Pay (card or ACH), with a review period: "Approve payment" or "Report a problem"
  • HO-12 — Cancel and get a refund minus only the accepted fee
  • HO-13 — Report a problem / dispute / credit / appeal
  • HO-14 — Uniform review request after the job (no review gating)
  • HO-15 — Post a job and receive bids, compare side by side, award (marketplace)
  • HO-16 — Remodeling project timeline, change orders, punch list
  • HO-17 — Home dashboard ("Home · My home · Jobs · Messages")

3. Pro / service company (P-A..P-D owner, CSR, dispatcher, technician; P-G..P-I staff) — DTA §2.1, §6.5

  • PR-01 — Company sign-up and onboarding: legal entity, trades, service area, team invites
  • PR-02 — Provider profile and coverage (trades, territory)
  • PR-03 — Upload license and insurance; expiry tracking; credential matrix by trade and territory
  • PR-04 — Eligibility: activate, restrict or suspend
  • PR-05 — Receive verified opportunities in waves; accept within a window; contact revealed after acceptance
  • PR-06 — "Today" view: new opportunities, today's jobs, unpaid invoices
  • PR-07 — Customer 360: contacts, timeline, merge duplicates
  • PR-08 — Lead pipeline: qualify, next action, won/lost
  • PR-09 — Contact center: call record, transcript, missed-call recovery
  • PR-10 — Dispatch: assign technician, vehicle, route; resolve conflicts
  • PR-11 — Job as a task with owner, due date, states and evidence
  • PR-12 — Field job execution: checklist, photos, time; close only with evidence
  • PR-13 — Inspection forms and diagnosis
  • PR-14 — Pricebook and good-better-best estimates; estimate presentation mode
  • PR-15 — Mobile offline sync for technicians
  • PR-16 — Team: employee skills, availability, time, payroll export
  • PR-17 — Subcontractors, scorecards, training/SOPs
  • PR-18 — Buy verified leads with caps, budgets and quality credits
  • PR-19 — Bid on posted jobs
  • PR-20 — Grow: website/forms, campaigns, local SEO, reviews (with BrandPilot)
  • PR-21 — Supplier pricing / parts from supplier partners on a pro's jobs

4. Company / property manager (P-F "James") — DTA §2.1, §6.5, §6.9

  • PM-01 — Delegated portfolio: open requests across properties
  • PM-02 — Approve estimates within a monetary mandate; escalate above it
  • PM-03 — Track vendor jobs and invoices per property; export history
  • PM-04 — Invite existing vendors into the network
  • PM-05 — Multi-company / multi-location hierarchy, enterprise branding
  • PM-06 — "Preferred pros and suppliers" list

5. Supplier (P-K "Maria") — DTA §2.1, §6.5, §6.9

  • SU-01 — Receive an authorized PO, confirm or propose a date, deliver, invoice
  • SU-02 — Supplier access by invitation from a provider (free)
  • SU-03 — Discovery of suppliers and partners (business network)
  • SU-04 — Supplier linked to an equipment warranty in the record
  • SU-05 — "Supplier connection on direct jobs"; reach pros at the moment of need; repeat orders

6. HomeGig (AI agent)

  • HG-01 — Homeowner AI agent: plain-language and photo intake, shortlists pros, suggests a time, owner approves each step
  • HG-02 — Job-post assistant
  • HG-03 — Governed AI plan, tool run under permission, verification, kill switch
  • HG-04 — AI Receptionist / Scheduling / Dispatch / Estimator / Follow-up / Collections / Review agents
  • HG-05 — Knowledge answers with citations, or abstains
  • HG-06 — Voice AI (push-to-talk)
  • HG-07 — Map of nearby pros
  • HG-08 — "Reminds you when care is due"

7. VaultGig (property record) = DTA "WG-P005 Home360" (M06, M07)

  • VG-01 — Register a property and the user's authority
  • VG-02 — Technical history: each job filed to the property (step 16)
  • VG-03 — Transfer an authorized history to a new owner (sale)
  • VG-04 — Equipment: model and serial
  • VG-05 — Link manual, warranty and supplier
  • VG-06 — Four-season maintenance plan and due reminders → "Book maintenance"
  • VG-07 — Photos, documents and evidence vault, shared with scope and expiry
  • VG-08 — Provider internal costs stay private in the record
  • VG-09 — Property attributes shared with InvesNetwork, read-only

8. Account / auth / privacy

  • AC-01 — Register account and organization; email confirmation
  • AC-02 — Sign in; MFA for Owner/Admin/Finance; recovery; lockout
  • AC-03 — Accept an invitation and select a workspace
  • AC-04 — Roles and per-action permissions; revoke and audit
  • AC-05 — Notification preferences and opt-out; retry failed delivery
  • AC-06 — Consent by purpose and channel; revocation cascades
  • AC-07 — Export or delete my data (with legal holds)
  • AC-08 — Revoke a device; protect local data
  • AC-09 — EN/ES bilingual from day one (design: EN only to start)
  • AC-10 — 18+ accounts only

9. Payments and admin

  • PA-01 — Invoice: deposit, progress, final; unique number; sales tax
  • PA-02 — Process, allocate and reconcile payments (never "Paid" from an unreconciled webhook)
  • PA-03 — Collections, adjustments, refunds, disputes
  • PA-04 — Payment hold and release; payout to pro
  • PA-05 — Membership (subscription) and usage metering; lead charges
  • PA-06 — Partner financing; accounting sync; cash-flow forecast
  • AD-01 — Company admin: users, branches, teams, crews
  • AD-02 — APIs, credentials and signed webhooks
  • AD-03 — Trust & safety console: provider verification queue, matching monitor, cases, elevated access
  • AD-04 — Fraud, compliance, sanctions and restore
  • AD-05 — KPI dashboards, goals; company and personal views down to evidence
  • AD-06 — Release, incident, rollback; code graph, health, capacity
  • AD-07 — Verified job outcome graph and verified action portfolio
